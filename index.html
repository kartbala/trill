Trill Craft · Pi Zero W · USB MIDI

Brass Creature

A metal sculpture you play by touching it. Each brass limb is one note of C harmonic minor.

Tap a limb to hear it
  1. TouchYour hand adds a tiny electrical charge to a brass limb. You don't have to press anything.
  2. SenseTrill Craft measures all 30 of its channels. One limb's reading jumps.
  3. DecideThe Pi reads the Craft over four I2C wires, 200 times a second. When a reading passes 0.20, it sends a MIDI "note on".
  4. PlayThe Mac sees the Pi as a USB MIDI keyboard, and GarageBand plays the note.
Inspired by Helen Leigh's sonic circuit sculptures for Bela. Built in Rotterdam, 2026.
