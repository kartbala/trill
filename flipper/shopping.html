Shopping briefing

Checked Wednesday 30 September, 15:40. For tomorrow's library session, Thursday 13:00 to 16:00.

One thing is a must: solder. Everything else lets you add the metal limbs tomorrow. Budget: about 8 euros for the must, about 15 euros with the limb wire, about 27 euros if you also need a wire stripper.

1. First choice: Hubo Kralingen

Oudedijk 171. Phone 010 412 5534. Open today until 17:30. Directories list 09:00 as the weekday opening time, so tomorrow morning also works. About 1.6 km east of the library and 2.2 km from home, in a straight line.

Call before you ride. The prices are from Hubo's website; I could not check the shelf in this shop.

2. Backup: Karwei Maashaven

Maashaven Zuidzijde 100, south of the river. Phone 010 316 6610. Open until 21:00 on weekdays. About 2.9 km from the library and 3.9 km from home, in a straight line.

Karwei sells the same kinds of wire, but I did not check those prices.

3. What to say

On the phone: "Hi, do you speak English? Do you have Griffon Print solder for electronics in stock? Soldeertin voor elektronica, met harskern."

In the shop: "Waar ligt het soldeertin voor elektronica?" It is usually next to the soldering irons.

4. Do not buy

Solder 3 mm thick, or labelled for water pipes (drinkwater, Resist). It is for plumbing.

Soldeervloeistof or S-39. That flux eats circuit boards.

Jumper wires. No shop nearby has them, and tomorrow you cut a Trill cable instead.

5. Not worth the ride

Praxis lists electronics solder for home delivery only. Action's website lists no solder at all. MediaMarkt has none.
