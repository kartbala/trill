Test the sound without the Pi

Press Start sound, then press the number keys 1 to 8 on the Mac keyboard. Each key plays one note. If you hear them, the Mac side works.

It says "No Pi yet"
  1. The cable must be in the Pi's middle socket. The end socket only takes power.
  2. Wait one full minute after plugging in. The Pi is slow to wake.
  3. If the Mac asks "Allow accessory to connect?", choose Allow.
  4. If Chrome asks to use MIDI devices, choose Allow.
  5. Still nothing? The cable may be a charging-only cable. Try another.
